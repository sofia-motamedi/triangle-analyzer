# Triangle Geometry Problem

Write a program that takes the coordinates of three vertices of a triangle as three valid and positive numbers from the user, and performs the following tasks:

### A. Triangle Validity Check
Determine whether these three points can form a triangle.
*   **Example 1 (Valid):** `x1=1, y1=1`, `x2=2, y2=2`, and `x3=2, y3=2` can be the vertices of a triangle.
*   **Example 2 (Invalid):** `x1=1, y1=1`, `x2=2, y2=2`, and `x3=3, y3=3` cannot be the vertices of a triangle (they are collinear).

### B. Area Calculation
If the points form a triangle, calculate its area.

### C. Triangle Type Classification
Determine the type of the triangle. The triangle can be:
*   Equilateral
*   Isosceles
*   Right-angled
*   Scalene
